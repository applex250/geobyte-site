Band response

Every slider scales one absorption feature. The curve is normalised reflectance, so the shape is what matters, not the absolute level.

Definition

RSI = ( band_mean / band_peak )
        - ( band shoulder / band peak )

Ratio of a reflectance shoulder to its absorption core. Positive peaks flag argillic and phyllic halos; the value plotted above is the strongest feature in the window.

Limitations, stated plainly

Atmosphere first. Ratios suppress illumination but not water vapour; run this on surface-reflectance products, not raw radiance.

Mixing is the norm. A pixel holding 30% clay still reads weak. Report the fraction, not just the flag.

Field check beats the plot. Every anomaly listed here has been walked or sampled before it enters the map.
